Privacy Notice

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

At InByte, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our digital invitation generator and RSVP services.

1. How Your Data is Handled

InByte is built with a secure cloud architecture. We use Firebase Firestore to store your personal invitation data safely in the cloud, linked directly to your authenticated Google account.

Any custom images you upload are securely stored using iCloud storage. Because your data is tied to your secured Gmail account login, your personal dashboard and invitation data remain entirely in your control and cannot be accessed by unauthorized users.

2. Information You Provide

When you create an invitation on your dashboard, you may input the following details:

  • Invitee names and custom personal messages
  • Event dates, times, and location names
  • Custom uploaded images and photos

3. Authentication and Storage

We do not use invasive third-party advertising trackers. We utilize secure authentication tokens via Google Workspace to keep your account logged in safely. Additionally, we use your browser's Local Storage strictly to save your User Interface preferences, such as your dark mode display settings.

4. Third-Party Integrations

To provide you with a seamless experience, our platform utilizes standard third-party services:

  • Firebase & Google Auth: For secure database hosting and user login authentication.
  • Google Maps: We embed a standard Google Maps iframe to display your selected event location visually.
  • iCloud Storage: For secure hosting of your uploaded invitation images.

5. Your Responsibilities

Because your invitations are saved to your personal account, you are responsible for keeping your Google account login secure. Anyone you send your generated link to will be able to view the contents of that specific invitation, so we advise sharing your links only with your intended guests.
