Privacy Notice

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

At InByte, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our digital invitation generator and RSVP services.

1. How Your Data is Handled

InByte is built with a privacy-first architecture. We do not use backend databases or remote servers to store your personal invitation data. When you create an invitation, all the details you input are mathematically encoded (via Base64 encryption) directly into the unique URL generated for you.

Because your data lives exclusively inside the link you share, our team cannot read, access, sell, monitor, or delete your invitation data. It remains entirely in your control.

2. Information You Provide

When you use our generator, you may input the following details:

  • Invitee names
  • Event dates and times
  • Location names
  • Custom personal messages

3. Local Storage and Tracking

We do not use invasive tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers. We utilize your browser's Local Storage strictly to save your User Interface preferences. Specifically, we store a small, anonymous text flag so our application remembers your preferred visual display mode during future visits.

4. Third-Party Integrations

To provide you with a seamless and interactive experience, our pages utilize standard third-party services:

  • Google Maps: We embed a standard Google Maps iframe to display your selected event location visually.
  • GitHub Pages: Our static website files are hosted securely via GitHub Pages, which may temporarily log standard web request data for security routing.

5. Your Responsibilities

Because your invitation data is stored directly in the URL rather than behind a password-protected account, you are responsible for keeping your generated links secure. We highly advise against posting your generated invitation links on public forums or social media feeds if you do not want strangers to view your private event details.
